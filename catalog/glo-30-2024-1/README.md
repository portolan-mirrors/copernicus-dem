# Copernicus DEM GLO-30, release 2024_1

The Copernicus DEM GLO-30 digital surface model, release 2024_1, as 26,475 items, one per 1° × 1° tile, at 1 arcsecond in latitude (about 30 m) with longitude spacing that widens towards the poles. Heights are in metres above the EGM2008 geoid (EPSG:3855) on WGS84 (EPSG:4326), RasterPixelIsPoint. Each item has five cloud-optimized GeoTIFFs: the elevation model and the height error, editing, filling and water body masks, plus the product XML. Every raster was re-encoded losslessly and checked pixel for pixel against its source. Tiles are trimmed by the last row and column they share with their south and east neighbours, matching the AWS Open Data copy. There are no tiles over open ocean. Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for access patterns.

## Processing Notes

26,248 tiles last changed in release 2019_2 or 2020_2 were read from the AWS Open Data copy (s3://copernicus-dem-30m, 2021 release), which holds those versions; the other 227 (188 changed in 2022_1, 38 in 2023_1 and 1 in 2024_1; 25 of the 2023_1 tiles are not on AWS at all) were read from the CDSE eodata archive. Each item's copdem:pixel_source and copdem:release_changed record which. Built by tools/bulk.py and tools/stac.py in the repository linked from the root catalog.
